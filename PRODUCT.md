# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mobile-first users betting virtual currency (P-coins) on real-world prediction markets. Users range from casual daily-login players to competitive power users tracking portfolios and leaderboard rankings.

## Product Purpose

A prediction market platform where users make forecasts on real-world outcomes (sports, crypto, politics, tech), earn P-coins through accurate predictions and daily logins, and compete on leaderboards.

## Brand Personality

Clean, trustworthy, and approachable. Professional but not cold. Transparent and safe, like a well-designed financial tool but friendly enough for casual daily use.

## Anti-references

- Overly simplified AI-generated styling — no generic "modern" design patterns
- Too many colors clashing together — restrained palette only
- Overly complicated or noisy UI — clarity and simplicity first
- Generic fintech dark mode (e.g., Robinhood clone aesthetic)

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over cleverness** — users should never wonder what something does
2. **Restrained palette** — one accent color, tinted neutrals, no rainbow interfaces
3. **Confidence through calm** — trustworthy design communicates safety for virtual money
4. **Mobile-first, not mobile-only** — every interaction works within thumb reach

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target. Text contrast minimum 4.5:1. Touch targets minimum 44x44px. Respect reduced motion preferences.
